# Story Factory

Use this reference for building complete "人生副本" episodes.

## Episode Lengths

### 60-90 Seconds

- 0-3s: disaster-preview hook
- 3-12s: ordinary identity and desire
- 12-25s: opportunity and trust shortcut
- 25-50s: sweetener then first crack
- 50-75s: fast collapse
- 75-90s: lesson and CTA

### 3-5 Minutes

- 0-5s: hook
- 5-30s: protagonist, relationship, money
- 30-75s: why the opportunity looks real
- 75-120s: early good signs
- 120-210s: three escalating problems
- 210-260s: point of no return
- 260-300s: loss, lesson, CTA

### 8-15 Minutes

- 0-10s: title-card disaster preview
- 10-90s: origin scene with social pressure
- 90-180s: decision-making and cost details
- 180-360s: early hope
- 360-600s: multi-layer trap revealed
- 600-780s: failed exit attempts
- 780-870s: aftermath and rebuilding
- 870-900s: distilled lesson and next-topic CTA

## Scene Ingredients

Use concrete, observable details:

- Money: transfer fee, deposit, loan, rent, inventory, penalty, medical bill, interest.
- Social pressure: old classmate, cousin, spouse, partner, boss, group chat, contract table.
- Status dream: be your own boss, buy a house, prove yourself, help family, escape factory work.
- False proof: screenshots, crowded store, daily turnover, polished franchise office, "official" training.
- First crack: missing certificate, shrinking traffic, delayed payment, broken equipment, weird contract clause.
- Collapse marker: empty register, blocked contact, overdue notice, maxed credit card, overnight bus station.

## AI Cartoon Visual Style

Default visual prompt style:

```text
Chinese grayscale comic storyboard, ordinary working-class protagonist, cinematic lighting, gritty paper texture, black and gray palette with one blue accent, expressive tired face, realistic urban details, dramatic shadows, horizontal 16:9 frame, no photorealistic celebrity likeness, no brand logo
```

For each scene, include:

- Character: age, clothes, expression, body language.
- Setting: convenience store, barbecue stall, warehouse, train station, loan office, family living room.
- Symbol: receipt, contract, phone screen, transfer sign, empty shelf, debt note.
- Mood: hope, unease, panic, numbness, regret, recovery.

## Emotional Retention Chain

Each 20-40 seconds should answer one question and open another:

- Why did he believe it?
- What made it look profitable?
- What was the first warning sign?
- Why did he not quit immediately?
- Who benefited from his decision?
- What did he lose besides money?
- What should the viewer remember?

## Ending Lessons

Use one clean sentence, not a lecture:

- "能赚钱的店，通常不会急着转让。"
- "熟人关系不能替代合同，更不能替代调查。"
- "所有让你快速翻身的机会，都要先问一句：谁急着退出？"
- "沉没成本不能参与重大决策。"
- "真正的风险，往往藏在你最想相信的那句话里。"
